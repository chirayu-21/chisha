Our Story 💌

“Sometimes the biggest stories begin with the smallest conversations.” ❤️

🌷 7 October 2025

The Message That Started Everything

If someone had told me on 7 October 2025 that one random conversation would turn into our story, I probably wouldn't have believed them. 🥹❤️

It all started because you were in charge of singing, and the faculty told me to connect with you because I was one of the singing contestants. 🎶

Then you texted me asking for my friend's number so you could contact me.

And little did you know 😂

“I’m the only one you’re searching for.” 🙈❤️

We started talking about my song and my performance.

It was such a normal conversation. Nothing dramatic. Nothing that looked like the beginning of something huge.

But secretly... I already had a little crush on you from the very first day. 🫣💗

🎤 8 October 2025

The Day You Showed Me You Cared

The next day was performance day.

You came late to college🥺, around noon, and I performed.

But I wasn't happy with the Miss Freshers results. 😔

I told you about it.

And instead of simply saying “don't worry,” you actually took me to the faculty member in charge.

You tried to help me.

Later that night, we talked again.

You told me that you weren't satisfied with the results either, even though there wasn't really anything you could do about it.

Maybe it was a small moment... but I noticed it. 🥺❤️

🎂 9 October 2025

Happy Birthday, Sir

Then came your birthday.

I found out it was your birthday and literally ran after you in the hall calling you “Sir” because faculty had told us juniors to call seniors sir or ma'am. 😂😭

I wished you.

And later that night I texted:

“Is the cake celebration done?” 🎂😂

That conversation became another long conversation.

You had recently gone through a breakup and weren't completely over it.

So we didn't rush into anything. We were friends. Just friends.

But meanwhile, my tiny little crush was getting bigger and bigger. 🫣❤️

📱 Those first few days

The Instagram Girl

Only around five or six days into our friendship, another girl messaged you on Instagram and told you that she had feelings for you.

And honestly? I was scared. 😭

I was scared that you might accept her🥺.

You told me about her. You asked for my advice. You listened to what I thought you should say.

And you didn't reply to her in the way I was afraid you might.

That meant more to me than you probably realized. 🥹

After that, our friendship became a routine.

Morning texts. 🌅
Afternoon conversations. ☀️
Evening talks. 🌆
Night conversations. 🌙

Sometimes we'd talk for almost the entire night. 😂❤️

📚 November

The Other Boy

Then came November. Your GATE exam was getting closer.

And while all of that was happening, another boy approached me.

I talked to him.

But the truth was...

My heart already had someone else.
You. ❤️

I had such a strong crush on you, but I didn't tell you.

Because our friendship was so pure to me. I was scared that if I said what I really felt, I might lose you.

So I tried to give you space.

I reduced the attention I gave you because I wanted you to concentrate on your studies.

I ignored some texts. I ignored some calls.

I tried to convince myself that I was doing the right thing.

But you kept coming back.

You kept texting. You kept calling. You kept looking for me.

And somewhere in those days, I realized that you had become attached to me too. 🥺❤️

After a few days, I stopped talking to that other boy.

And I came back to talking to you. Properly.

And somehow, we went right back to those endless conversations.

Sometimes we'd talk for the entire night. 🌙🫶🏻

🌳 College Days

You Waited For Me

You were in your final year. I was in my first year.

And still, you would come to college just to meet me.

Sometimes you'd wait for hours while I attended lectures. 🥹

And once I was finally free, we'd walk around the college garden, talk, laugh and just spend time together. 🌸

People started noticing.

Then came the rumors.

People started talking about us. Even faculty knew. 👀😂

But somehow, none of that stopped us from choosing each other.

And yes... we both became a little possessive too. 😂❤️

✨ February — SORCE

The Day I Saw You Differently

Then came the three-day SORCE event.

You were the president of the department🙈. And I wasn't even supposed to attend because I was a first-year student.

But I still found little opportunities to see you. 🥹

And then came the last day.

You were wearing your blazer. Your hair was long. You had your sunglasses on. 😎

And I don't know what exactly happened in that moment... but when I looked at you, something felt different.

I remember thinking that maybe...

I was actually falling in love with you. ❤️

You played box cricket and I watched you.

Later, you were completely exhausted and rested your head on the table near me.

And somehow that tiny moment stayed in my heart. 🥺💗

🎭 Sparsh

You Became My Partner

Then came our annual function, Sparsh.

I was participating in anchoring, singing and the fashion show.

But there was one problem...

I didn't have a partner.

So you became my partner. 🥹❤️

You were already in your final year. You've already got the title. You didn't really need to participate in everything anymore.

But you still did the fashion show.

And that day, you somehow became my personal assistant. 😂😭❤️

You carried my heels. 👠
You brought me water. 💧
You helped with my meal. 🍱
You carried my lipstick. 💄
You kept track of my mobile. 📱

You were basically carrying everything except me. 😂🫶🏻

✈️ 16 February → 20 February

The Days We Couldn't Practice

You went to Chennai with your friends on 16 February. You came back on 20 February.

And suddenly we realized...

WE HAD BARELY PRACTICED OUR RAMP WALK. 😭😂

Our costumes weren't even ready.

So we decided we'd practice at your home.

On the evening of 20 February, I went to your house.

We had chicken biryani. 🍗🍚❤️

And then... practice?

Absolutely not. 😂

We were too full.

So we did timepass instead. Talked. Relaxed. And eventually slept.

🌙 21 February → 22 February

The Beginning of Us

The next day, 21 February, we went to college.

And somehow... we still hadn't practiced properly. 😂😭

So I went to your home again.

We had dinner.

You said you were going to sleep. You asked if you could rest with your head on my lap.

We were getting closer🥰.

Closer and closer..that we had some unforgettable moments together.

Distance between us was barely visible. more closer...

There was a moment when everything suddenly felt different.

I got scared because I didn't want to lose the friendship we had built.

And then you asked if we should stop. You gave me the choice.

And I realized... I didn't want to run away from what I was feeling.

After midnight, it became 22 February. 🌙❤️

And that became the day we officially became US. 🥹❤️

Our first kiss. Our first real promise to be together. Our beginning. 💗

🏸🎯 Little Things

Our Everyday Memories

We played badminton. 🏸

We cheered for each other.

I played carrom. 🎯

You cheered for me.

And I won. 😌😂❤️

It wasn't really about badminton or carrom. It was about having someone beside me who genuinely wanted to see me happy.

🛍️ 24 February

Costumes, Stalls & Little Things

On 24 February, we went to the market for costumes.

You helped me with the things I needed for my college stall too.

Another ordinary day. Another memory. Another little piece of us. 🥹💗

🌟 A Little Push From You

You Believed In Me

You encouraged me to file my nomination for college positions.

You pushed me to believe that I could actually do it.

And eventually, I got selected as the girls' representative. 🥹✨

You believed in me. Sometimes even when I wasn't sure about myself.

And I'll always remember that. ❤️

👗 28 February

Fashion Show Day

On fashion show day, we met at your home.

My dress wasn't ready.

And somehow, you helped me get ready. 🥹❤️

Then we both went and did the fashion show.

After everything was over... you went home. I went back to the hostel.

And somewhere between the singing event, birthday wishes, Instagram conversations, late-night talks, college gardens, rumors, SORCE, Sparsh, chicken biryani, unpracticed ramp walks, badminton, carrom, costumes and countless tiny moments...

Our love story had begun. ❤️

🌧️ April

When Things Got Difficult

Not every part of our story has been easy.

In April, your family found out about us.

They wanted us to separate and didn't want us to stay in contact.

And suddenly, something that had felt so beautiful and simple became difficult.

There were ups and downs. There were nights of overthinking. There were tears. There were moments where everything felt uncertain. 😔

We had to face things neither of us had expected.

But through all of it, we still cared about each other.

We're still figuring things out. Still growing. Still learning. Still trying to understand what the future looks like.

But no matter how complicated things became, all those little memories stayed with me.
